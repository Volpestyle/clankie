# Changelog

Published Clankie service releases, newest first. Each entry links to its
release notes and downloads. Tags that never produced a published release
are not listed. The companion app has its own releases.

These entries describe what shipped at the time. For current setup and
support, use [Get started](/get-started/) and the [reference](/reference/).

## v0.4.11 — 23 October 2026

A service update. This release publishes a
[change comparison](https://github.com/Volpestyle/clankie/compare/v0.4.10...v0.4.11)
rather than a written feature summary.
[Release and downloads](https://github.com/Volpestyle/clankie/releases/tag/v0.4.11).

## v0.4.9 — 22 October 2026

Improved clean-Mac installation, moving Clankie between computers, and hiring
workers on joined machines. Replaces the unpublished v0.4.7 and v0.4.8 builds.
[Release notes and downloads](https://github.com/Volpestyle/clankie/releases/tag/v0.4.9).

## v0.4.6 — 17 October 2026

Clearer project working rules and fleet results, with better recovery when
workers, computers or accounts stop answering. Replaces the unpublished
v0.4.5 build.
[Release notes and downloads](https://github.com/Volpestyle/clankie/releases/tag/v0.4.6).

## v0.4.4 — 11 October 2026

Added Clankie's built-in work tracker and evidence storage, more coding
harnesses, machine access levels and shared resource controls. Service bundles
cover Apple silicon macOS and arm64 and x64 Linux.
[Release notes and downloads](https://github.com/Volpestyle/clankie/releases/tag/v0.4.4).

## v0.3.3 — 30 September 2026

Native Claude and Codex seats, remote worker connections, direct Mac pairing,
voice improvements and persona image references.
[Release notes and downloads](https://github.com/Volpestyle/clankie/releases/tag/v0.3.3).

## v0.2.1 — 20 September 2026

Fixed delivered-file responses so strict CLI and app clients accept a
successful file publication.
[Release notes and downloads](https://github.com/Volpestyle/clankie/releases/tag/v0.2.1).

## v0.2.0 — 20 September 2026

The first downloadable Apple silicon service bundle: a local agent, terminal
console, Herdr workers and encrypted app transport.
[Release notes and downloads](https://github.com/Volpestyle/clankie/releases/tag/v0.2.0).

For the current release and its requirements, use the
[release list](https://github.com/Volpestyle/clankie/releases).
