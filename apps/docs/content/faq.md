# Questions

Short answers to what people ask first. Clankie (clankie.bot) is not affiliated
with clankie.com.

## What is Clankie?

Clankie is a personal AI assistant with a personality that runs on your own
computer, remembers you, and leads coding agents like Claude Code and Codex
until their work lands. He plans the work, hires agents into your
[Herdr](https://herdr.dev) sessions, checks what they finish, and helps with
everyday and creative work between jobs. See [how he works](/how-it-works/).

## Is Clankie a Discord bot?

No: Clankie is an agent that runs on your machine, and Discord is one optional
place to reach him. He joins your server through a Discord bot you create and
own; there is no shared official bot. You can also talk to him in his console,
in Claude Code or Codex, and in the iPhone and iPad app. See
[Discord and play setup](/diy/#hang-out-and-play).

## What does Clankie cost?

The software is free, and so is the Clankie account that lets your phone reach
your machine. Model providers and the services you connect may charge; Clankie
uses your own subscriptions and keys. See [get started](/get-started/).

## Can I run Clankie on my own Mac?

Yes, and only there: Clankie runs on machines you own, never on ours. He
installs on Apple silicon Macs with macOS 14 or newer and on arm64 or x64 Linux;
a Windows PC joins as a fleet machine. The app reaches your machine through the
clankie.bot gateway, which carries encrypted traffic it cannot read. See
[install](/get-started/#diy-install-on-your-own-machine).

## Is Clankie open source?

Yes: Clankie's source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Volpestyle/clankie)
under Apache-2.0, except the native Discord voice and media process, which is
AGPL-3.0-or-later. The iPhone and iPad app is a free client.
