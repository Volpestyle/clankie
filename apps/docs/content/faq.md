# Questions

Short answers to what people ask first. Clankie (clankie.bot) is not affiliated
with clankie.com.

## What is Clankie?

Clankie is an open-source, self-hosted super agent platform. It runs always-on
leads on your own machines: Grok Bot, Claude Code, Codex, or the built-in lead.
Leads hire coding workers into [Herdr](https://herdr.dev) terminals across your
machines, and the app shows you the work. See [how it works](/how-it-works/).

## How is it different from a ticket board?

You talk to a lead that is already running, instead of filing work into a queue.
The lead keeps the context, staffs the job, answers its workers and tells you
what landed. Tracking stays wherever you already keep it: Linear, GitHub issues,
files or the built-in tracker.

## Is Clankie a Discord bot?

No: Clankie runs on your machine, and Discord is one optional place to reach its
built-in lead. That lead joins your server through a Discord bot you create and
own; there is no shared official bot. You can also talk to leads in the console,
in Claude Code or Codex, and in the iPhone and iPad app. See
[Discord and play setup](/diy/#hang-out-and-play).

## What does Clankie cost?

The software is free, and so is the optional Clankie account that lets your
phone reach your machine from anywhere. Model providers and the services you connect may charge; Clankie
uses your own subscriptions and keys. See [get started](/get-started/).

## Can I run Clankie on my own Mac?

Yes, and only there: Clankie runs on machines you own, never on ours. It
installs on Apple silicon Macs with macOS 14 or newer and on arm64 or x64 Linux;
a Windows PC joins as a fleet machine. The app reaches your machine directly on
your own network, or from anywhere through the clankie.bot gateway, which carries
encrypted traffic it cannot read. See
[install](/get-started/#diy-install-on-your-own-machine).

## Is Clankie open source?

Yes: Clankie's source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Volpestyle/clankie)
under Apache-2.0, except the native Discord voice and media process, which is
AGPL-3.0-or-later. The iPhone and iPad app is a free client whose source is
private.
