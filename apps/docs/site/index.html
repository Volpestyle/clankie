A persistent agent with a life of his own

Clankie.

He lives on your Mac. He hangs out in your Discord in text and voice, plays Pokémon live on a watch surface, draws pictures, makes videos, browses the web, remembers people, codes, and leads a fleet of coding agents you can watch.

Nothing of his runs in the cloud. The iPhone and iPad app is a window into your machine.

What he does

One Clankie, everywhere he is.

The console, Discord, voice, and gameplay are rooms, not separate agents. He can read his other rooms, and his persona is owner-authored — never caller input.

Discord teammate

A real member of your server: bounded text turns, consented group voice, pictures riding his replies, and memory of the people he talks to. Silence is a real answer.

Plays games on stream

Pokémon FireRed and Emerald from his own seat in a hosted PokeAgents world, watchable live in a Discord Activity with voice commentary over the run.

Makes things

Image and video generation behind one provider-neutral seam (OpenAI, Google, Grok), and a browser with his own accounts.

Codes and leads

The same coding tools every agent has, plus the herdr CLI for fanning work out to visible agent panes. No mission protocol — he delegates, watches, and reports what actually happened.

01 · Start

Install him on this Mac.

An Apple-silicon Mac running macOS 14 or newer. The console is a complete way to talk to him; remote access is an option, not a requirement.

Install and open him

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Volpestyle/clankie/main/install.sh | sh
clankie

The launcher starts the service and opens the console.

Give him a model

In the console, /auth stores a provider key or OAuth login in the Mac’s Keychain-backed credential broker, and /model picks the model he thinks with. That is the whole setup — talk to him. /persona, /image-model, /discord, and /connect are optional and can wait. A local runtime (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM) works too: /provider → “add a local endpoint”.

Know what is configured

clankie doctor
clankie status

clankie doctor prints the install card: kind, version, model, credentials by id, optional tools, and what to fix. Every configuration the console offers is also a headless command — see the CLI reference.

Use the terminal you already like

The console runs in an ordinary terminal or inside a multiplexer. The supported fleet integration is Herdr: link his plugin once and he can lead, watch, and steer visible agent panes from the console.

02 · App

Set up the Mac once. Carry the window with you.

The iPhone and iPad app reaches your Mac through a narrow public doorway. It does not move him into a hosted service; the Mac remains the host you return to from anywhere.

  1. Sign the Mac in

    Run /gateway, choose Enable remote access, enter the email your invitation named, then the one-time code it receives. There are no URLs, host ids, or tokens to copy.

  2. Keep him running

    Run clankie autostart enable. He and his relay now start at login. Leave the Mac awake, online, and signed in if you want him reachable while you are away.

  3. Install the app

    From your TestFlight invitation, and open it.

  4. Pair the phone

    Run clankie pair on the Mac, then scan the QR code or type its single-use code in the app. Review the access it offers and connect. clankie devices lists and revokes paired devices.

Hybrid

Move between the console and the app. Both are windows into the same Clankie and Mac.

App-first

After setup, spend nearly all your time in Messages, the fleet, and terminal observation on iPhone or iPad. The Mac quietly remains the host.

You do not need Tailscale. App users never copy URLs, host ids, or bearer tokens, and they do not need to understand Herdr unless they want fleet panes. The network page lists the exact public routes.

03 · Underneath

Your Mac remains the center.

He is one local service plus the surfaces that reach it. The full shape is on How he works; the short version is below.

On your Mac

Clankie, his model credentials, every conversation, his memory, Herdr panes, terminal processes, and the owner settings that shape him. HTTP on 127.0.0.1:4310.

At the doorway

Account discovery, authenticated Mac presence, short-lived pairing routes, and request transit. No model, no terminal, no message store.

Read the privacy notice for data handling and the public network surface for what the internet can reach.
