The Clankie docs

Leads you talk to.
Work you can see.

Clankie is an open-source, self-hosted super agent platform. Run always-on leads on your own machines: Grok Bot, Claude Code, Codex, or the built-in lead. They hire coding workers across your machines, and you see the work in one place.

New here? See how it works, or find your path below.

Run it yourself

Open source. Your machines, your models, your tools. Begin on a Mac or Linux machine and grow from there; a Windows PC joins as a fleet machine.

See the work

Sessions first. A little village underneath.

Talk to a lead in Sessions, where every agent sits under the machine it runs on. The Feed holds what needs you and what finished. When a job grows into a team, the World shows who's working and opens the conversation behind each figure. Terminal is there when you want a closer look.

The app reaches Clankie on the machine you run it on. Available features depend on that setup and your device's access.
