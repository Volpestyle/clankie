---
description: Diagnose and recover a blocked or failed multi-agent mission without discarding evidence or bypassing policy.
---

Freeze new side effects. Identify the first causal failure rather than the loudest downstream symptom. Preserve worker output, terminal tail, branch, diff, environment metadata, and correlation identifiers. Classify the failure as requirement, planning, implementation, integration, infrastructure, provider, credential, policy, or verification. Add the smallest recovery task with a named owner and explicit success criteria. Prefer a different worker or harness when the failure may be agent-specific. Re-run the unchanged failing check after repair, then run the broader regression gate. Record the incident and any proposed doctrine change separately.
