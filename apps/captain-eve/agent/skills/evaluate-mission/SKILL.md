---
description: Evaluate whether a mission demonstrates reliable lead-agent orchestration rather than merely producing an artifact.
---

Use the event log, plan, doctrine hash, worker identities, approvals, command evidence, diffs, tests, and final authoritative state. Score goal success, plan validity, independent verification, defect detection, recovery routing, policy adherence, evidence completeness, observability, cost, and latency. Critical failures cannot be averaged away. Compare against a baseline when proposing prompt, skill, routing, or doctrine changes. Keep the change only when the target metric improves without regressions.
