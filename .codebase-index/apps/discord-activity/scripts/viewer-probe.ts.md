# apps/discord-activity/scripts/viewer-probe.ts

Tiny evidence probe: attaches as a real viewer
(CLANKIE_ACTIVITY_VIEWER_URL, default
ws://127.0.0.1:4320/frames), counts frames and
overlays for CLANKIE_VIEWER_SECONDS (default 90),
logs each overlay's JSON, and prints the totals —
proof of what actually arrives on the surface.
