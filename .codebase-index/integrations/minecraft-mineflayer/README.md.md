Package guide: the loopback-only boundary
(literal 127.0.0.1/::1, service-private auth,
no combat/commands/chat capabilities), the
pinned Mineflayer 4.37.1 / pathfinder 2.4.5 /
Paper 1.21.11 stack, and the frozen
collect-craft-place proof where the
console-only Paper verifier — not Mineflayer
completion — is the success authority.
Documents the operator commands:
`minecraft:paper:bootstrap` (pinned download,
EULA not accepted), `minecraft:readiness`, and
the `minecraft:live-proof` run with its
receipt discipline. References ADRs 0016 and 0044.
