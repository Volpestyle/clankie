# scenarios/minecraft/collect-craft-place/v1

Version 1 of the frozen Minecraft fixture. Five
files: scenario.yml (the task definition),
server.properties (the pinned lab server
policy), one .sha256 pin for each, and
binding.json (environmentKind minecraft_java,
characterId clankie, worldId
private-paper-world, lane gameplay, repeating
the scenario digest as fixtureSha256).

The task: player Clankie, on a flat Paper 1.21.11
world with seed 774042, collects at least 8 logs
(oak/birch/spruce), crafts a crafting table, and
places it inside the target cuboid (-2..2, 65..67,
6..10), within 300 seconds and 256 events.
reset-blocks lists the eight OAK_LOG positions
restored on reset. The server is loopback-only
(127.0.0.1:25574), offline-mode, whitelisted,
peaceful, no structures/mobs — laboratory-only.
