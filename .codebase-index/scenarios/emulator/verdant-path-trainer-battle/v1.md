# scenarios/emulator/verdant-path-trainer-battle/v1

Version 1 of the frozen GBA trainer-battle
fixture: scenario.json (the deterministic world
and win condition), scenario.sha256 (pin of its
exact bytes), and binding.json (ties that digest
to the gba_emulator environment, characterId
clankie, lane gameplay).

The scenario: on an 8x6 fictional map
(verdant-path) with two blocked tiles, the player
starts at (0,1) with a two-mon party (embercub
lv8, leafling lv7), walks to rival-mira at (4,1),
wins the battle against her sproutlet lv6, and
reaches target (3,1). Determinism anchors: core
gba-core-double-v1, savestate identity digest,
RNG seed 20260719. Evidence is bounded at 64
events and 64 decisions; expected outcome is
battleResult "won" with at least 6 decisions.
